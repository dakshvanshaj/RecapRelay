## The problem this solves

The moment a call ends is when the deal is most alive in your head — and exactly then, writing it up competes
with the next call, the next email, the rest of the day. So it waits. And once it waits, pieces of it start to go:

- **What was actually said gets thinner with every hour that passes**, until "update the CRM" becomes a rushed
  two-line note days later, if it happens at all.
- **A commitment made out loud mid-call** — "I'll send that by Friday," "let's reconnect Monday" — has nothing
  forcing it to become a tracked task once the call ends; it just lives in someone's memory until it doesn't.
- **A risk signal mentioned in passing** — budget pushback, a competitor's name, a timeline slipping — vanishes
  along with the call itself unless someone happens to flag it.

RecapRelay reads the transcript the moment it's available, writes the recap and every action item to your sheet,
and tells the owner immediately — so nothing depends on someone remembering to type it up later.
